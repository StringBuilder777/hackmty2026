# SynchroData demo storyboard

**Format:** 1920×1080 landscape, 24 seconds.
**Audio:** Spanish TTS narration, no music; restrained interface clicks implied by motion.
**VO direction:** clear, calm Spanish delivery; confident but never alarmist.
**Style basis:** DESIGN.md and the local SynchroData capture.

## Global direction

The video moves through a light, high-clarity product world. Blue path lines, cards and signal chips create motion; white surfaces remain readable. Each beat uses a soft blue radial glow, structured panels, a source label and one active accent. Scene handoffs use a sweeping blue route line that becomes the next frame's divider.

## Beat 1 — The missing context (0–6s)

**VO cue:** “Antes de comprar, un enlace no basta. SynchroData reúne las señales que importan.”

**Concept:** A product page slides into focus behind a crisp blue route glyph. The viewer immediately understands that a link is only the starting point.

**Visuals:** Full-page capture inside a floating browser card; oversized “COMPRA CON CONTEXTO” headline; blue path line; three signal chips; logo.

**Choreography:** Browser card rises with subtle perspective, headline types in by word, chips cascade, path line draws from left to right. A blue route wipe carries into the next beat.

## Beat 2 — Evidence, not guesses (6–12s)

**VO cue:** “Analiza producto, vendedor y dominio, y muestra la evidencia disponible sin prometer certezas.”

**Concept:** The interface resolves into three evidence cards. The accent blue connects a URL, seller and domain while a caveat remains visible.

**Visuals:** URL pill, product card, seller card, domain card, blue connector line, source label and “EVIDENCIA DISPONIBLE” stamp.

**Choreography:** URL types on, cards slide from staggered directions, connectors draw, evidence stamp settles. A route-line sweep transitions forward.

## Beat 3 — Compare and plan (12–18s)

**VO cue:** “Compara opciones, revisa variantes y organiza las paradas de tu recorrido.”

**Concept:** Three offer cards align into a clear decision, then collapse into two route stops connected by a blue itinerary line.

**Visuals:** price comparison cards, matching-variant labels, compact map grid, pins and route line.

**Choreography:** prices count upward, selected card gains blue edge, pins drop, route line draws between them. A circular evidence pulse opens the audit beat.

## Beat 4 — Investigate with traceability (18–24s)

**VO cue:** “Para investigar más a fondo, la auditoría forense transforma datos en hallazgos con evidencia rastreable. SynchroData. Compra con más contexto.”

**Concept:** The consumer view expands into a forensic case file: a reported pattern, exposure amount and evidence chips, ending on the brand.

**Visuals:** Phantom Vendor finding card, exposure amount, evidence IDs, source trail and centered SynchroData lockup.

**Choreography:** finding card resolves from a scan line, evidence IDs stamp in, source trail draws, then every UI element settles into the final brand lockup and fades gently.
