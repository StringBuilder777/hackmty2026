# SynchroData Design Reference

## Overview

SynchroData is a light, precise product interface. It uses a soft gray canvas, white rounded cards, thin cool-gray borders, and a confident electric-blue accent for decisions and navigation. The presentation is calm and evidence-led rather than alarmist.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#F5F5F7` — main background.
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — cards and panels.
- **Primary text**: `#1D1D1F` — headings and data.
- **Secondary text**: `#64748B` — explanatory copy.
- **Blue action**: `#1463D8` — calls to action and active states.
- **Blue tint**: `#E6EDF6` — selected surfaces.
- **Blue border**: `#ADC5DD` — quiet emphasis.
- **Alert amber**: `#D97706` — caveats and review states.
- **Alert red**: `#DC2626` — suspicious-simulation marker only.

## Typography

- **Geist / system sans**: all product copy, headings and numeric labels.
- **Geist Mono / ui-monospace**: source, signal and audit labels.
- Headline treatment: heavy, compact, high contrast; supporting text stays measured and readable.

## Elevation

White cards float above the gray canvas with soft, diffused shadows and one-pixel `#E2E8F0` borders. Depth comes from layered cards and localized blue glows, never dark full-frame gradients.

## Components

- **Evidence selector**: rounded product/site options with blue active outline.
- **Analysis bar**: pale-blue input strip and solid blue action button.
- **Signal chips**: compact icon + label pills.
- **Comparison cards**: restrained panels with price, source and state.
- **Forensic finding**: labeled pattern, exposure amount and evidence trail.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Preserve the soft light canvas and white card system.
- Use `#1463D8` only to direct attention and show progress.
- Keep signals explicit and bounded; use source labels and caveats.
- Use crisp rounded geometry with generous whitespace.

### Don'ts

- Do not use dark cinematic gradients or neon cyber-security visuals.
- Do not use red as a global theme; reserve it for the suspicious marker.
- Do not present a risk score as proof of fraud.
- Do not use dense, tiny data tables in hero frames.
